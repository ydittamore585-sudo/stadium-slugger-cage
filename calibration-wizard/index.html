Calibrate your cage (once, ~10 min)

Your phone sits on a tripod and never moves. You tell the wizard where a few known points are in the camera view. From that, it learns to turn pixels into feet on your field — the foundation every tracked number stands on.

You'll need

  • 📱 Phone on a tripod, facing the cage (don't move it after this)
  • 📏 Tape measure — cage length × width, camera height
  • ⚾ A ball, and something tall with a known height (bat on a tee, yardstick)
  • 🚧 2–4 cones or marks at known distances (15 ft / 30 ft etc.) — optional but helps

The honesty contract

  • This wizard never invents numbers. If the evidence is thin, it says so.
  • Until verification passes, every projected number stays labeled uncalibrated estimate.
  • Verification failing is a result, not an error — re-tap and try again.
